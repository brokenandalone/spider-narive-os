# Spider Art Lab: approved creative media requirements
Concept only, 2026-10-15. Unique amber/lilac background and art icon are designed; painting functions are not implemented.

- Graphite pencils: H, 2H, HB, B, 2B, 4B, 6B, 8B; pressure/tilt response, tooth, hardness and smudge.
- Blendable colored pencils: stacking, pressure, pigment buildup, color mixing, burnishing and blender tools.
- Alcohol and water-based markers: nib shape, marker overlap, transparency, bleed and blending.
- Paint types: watercolor, gouache, acrylic, oil, ink, airbrush with medium-specific texture and brushes.
- Blenders: blending stump/tortillon, soft blending brushes, smudge, blur and wet pigment blending.
- Photo-to-outline and stencil generator: import photo, adjustable edge/contour detection, remove background, clean and vector/raster export. Never delete or overwrite the input photo.
- Tattoo mode: stencil line work, shading, body-placement mockup, high DPI printable output.
- Layered non-destructive editing, undo history, stylus/tablet support, color management, raster and vector export.
- Commercial engine quality and proper third-party license and sample/brush rights audits.
