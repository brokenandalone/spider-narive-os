Play instruments

Availability

0.15 private beta. No public checkout yet.

Current development targets Linux desktop musicians. This build is available through the owner's development workflow while we finish sound-device qualification, redistribution licensing, stability checks and production support.

Prices, subscriptions, plugins, audio-interface support and a public release date have not been announced. This preview intentionally has no purchase or payment flow.

Audio is processed locally in supported offline workflows; separately installed optional engines may have different capabilities or requirements. User recordings are not bundled in the application's release archive.
